🔬 Module 1: The Combustion Science Challenge

On Earth, fire behavior is dominated by gravity-driven buoyancy: hot combustion gases expand, decrease in density, and rise rapidly. This buoyant updraft supplies fresh oxygen to the flame base and stretches the flame upward into the familiar teardrop shape.

In spaceflight (microgravity and partial gravity), this physics completely transforms:

Zero Gravity (ISS Microgravity, 0g)
Natural convection vanishes entirely ($Gr = 0$). Diffusion flames become symmetrical spheres. Without buoyant venting, hot soot and combustion products pool around the flame zone. Stagnant flames starve of oxygen and self-extinguish unless ventilation fans supply forced airflow.
Partial Gravity (Moon at 0.166g, Mars at 0.38g)
Buoyant acceleration is partially restored (16% on Moon, 38% on Mars). Under classical Roper buoyancy scaling ($L_f \propto g^{-0.25}$), flames elongate axially by up to 58%, increasing radiant heat exposure to surrounding habitation modules.
Elevated Oxygen Habitats (Moon Base: 34% O₂ at 10.2 psi)
To minimize pre-breathing time before spacesuit EVAs, future lunar habitats will operate at intermediate pressure ($10.2\text{ psi}$) and elevated oxygen ($30\text{--}34\%\text{ O}_2$). Materials burn based on Oxygen Partial Pressure ($P_{\text{O}_2} = P \times \text{O}_2\%$). At $3.47\text{ psi } P_{\text{O}_2}$, materials are $\approx 1.18\times$ more flammable than at sea level.

🚀 Module 3: Step-by-Step Analytical Workflow

Step 1: Test a Flight Scenario in the Extrapolator

In the Partial-Gravity Flammability Extrapolator, select a preset button such as 🌕 Moon Base Habitat or drag the sliders:

  • Gravity Slider (0g to 1g): Controls Grashof buoyancy acceleration.
  • Oxygen Slider (10% to 100%): Sets oxidizer volume fraction.
  • Pressure Slider (2 to 20 psi): Sets total cabin atmospheric pressure.
P_O2 = P_total × (O2% / 100) = 10.2 psi × 0.34 = 3.47 psi (Normoxic Elevated)
Flame Height: L_f(0.16g) = 30 mm × (0.16)^(-0.25) = 47.4 mm (+58%)

Step 2: Ask the AI Scenario Analyzer

Click any quick prompt pill (e.g., 🌕 Lunar Base Solid Flammability) or type a natural language inquiry into the input box and click Run AI Analysis.

The system executes a 4-step live pipeline:

  1. Extracts flight parameters ($g$, $\text{O}_2\%$, $P$) via sub-millisecond regex and semantics.
  2. Calculates deterministic physics bounds (Roper scaling, $P_{\text{O}_2}$, Grashof convection).
  3. Retrieves relevant NASA PSI investigation datasets via Hybrid FAISS + BM25 search.
  4. Synthesizes a peer-reviewed, physics-bounded hypothesis with explicit source citations and confidence metrics.

Step 3: Compare Any Two Investigations Side-by-Side

Click ⚖️ Compare Any 2 Investigations in the header of the investigation explorer. Select two experiments (e.g., BASS vs BASS-II or FLEX vs CFI) and click Compare. The matrix displays side-by-side fuel specifications, atmospheric conditions, hardware racks, and computes knowledge graph relationship paths.
