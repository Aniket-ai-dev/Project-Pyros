PHYSIOLOGICAL TRADE-OFFS & FLAMMABILITY SURGE

Spacesuits vs. Moon Base vs. CFI-G

Balancing Decompression Sickness ("The Bends") Against Material Flammability
SLIDE 05 / 07

ATMOSPHERIC REGIMES & PARTIAL PRESSURE COMPARISON

• Earth Sea Level (Baseline): \(14.7\text{ psi}\) • \(21.0\%\text{ O}_2\) • \(P_{\text{O}_2} = 3.09\text{ psi}\) • \(1.00\times\) Flammability Index
• Moon Base & Rover (Artemis): \(10.2\text{ psi}\) • \(34.0\%\text{ O}_2\) • \(P_{\text{O}_2} = 3.47\text{ psi}\) • \(1.18\times\) Flammability (\(>40\%\) \(\text{O}_2\) increase)
• Spacesuit EVA (High Mobility): \(4.3\text{ psi}\) • \(100.0\%\text{ O}_2\) • \(P_{\text{O}_2} = 4.30\text{ psi}\) • \(1.59\times\) Flammability (Hyperoxic Surge)
• ACME CFI-G (ISS Flight Record): \(14.7\text{ psi}\) • \(85.0\%\text{ O}_2\) • \(P_{\text{O}_2} = 12.49\text{ psi}\) • \(7.08\times\) Flammability Limit

THE PRE-BREATHE DILEMMA & THE ENGINEERING RATIONALE

1. Why 4.3 psi in spacesuits? Depressurization is mandatory so astronauts can bend finger joints and limbs against suit tension. \(100\%\) pure \(\text{O}_2\) is required to keep alveolar oxygen high enough to sustain life.

2. The Pre-Breathe Purge: To prevent decompression sickness ("the bends"), astronauts must pre-breathe \(100\%\text{ O}_2\) for hours at \(14.7\text{ psi}\) to expel bloodstream nitrogen.

3. The Moon Base Solution: Operating at \(10.2\text{ psi}\) with \(34\%\text{ O}_2\) slashes pre-breathing to minutes, enabling routine EVAs. However, this \(>40\%\) increase in oxygen concentration drastically accelerates flame spread and lowers material ignition thresholds.

DETERMINISTIC PHYSICS: BRIDGING 0g AND 1g

• Roper Flame Height Scaling: \(L_f(g) = L_f(1\text{g}) \cdot g^{-0.25}\) — Earth (\(1\text{g}\)): \(30\text{ mm}\) | Mars (\(0.38\text{g}\)): \(38.2\text{ mm}\) (\(+27\%\)) | Moon (\(0.166\text{g}\)): \(47.0\text{ mm}\) (\(+57\%\)) | \(0\text{g}\): Asymptotically bounded spherical dome (\(\sim 75\text{ mm}\)).
• Grashof Convective Draft: \(u_{\text{conv}} \propto \sqrt{2 \cdot g \cdot \beta \cdot \Delta T \cdot L}\) — Earth: \(0.55\text{ m/s}\) (buoyant flicker) | Moon: \(0.23\text{ m/s}\) (\(-59\%\)) | \(0\text{g}\): \(0.00\text{ m/s}\) (pure molecular diffusion, high risk of quiescent smoldering).
• ACME E-FIELD Ionic Mimicry: Electric fields applied across flames exert Lorentz body forces on ions (\(\text{CHO}^+\), \(\text{H}_3\text{O}^+\)), experimentally mimicking partial-gravity buoyant acceleration in \(0\text{g}\).
• ACME BRE Emulation: Burning Rate Emulator used gaseous fuels in still air to emulate condensed solid materials flammability across thermal conductivity and thickness variables.
